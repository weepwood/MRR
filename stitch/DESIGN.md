# Design System

This document outlines the core aesthetic and functional principles of our design system, derived directly from our design tokens.

## Theme

- **Color Mode**: light

## Colors

Our brand colors are precisely defined to ensure consistency and impact across all applications.

- **Primary Color**: `#1275e2` (Used for primary actions, main branding elements, and key interactive components.)
- **Secondary Color**: `#5f78a3` (Supports the primary color, suitable for less prominent UI elements, complementary graphics, and secondary actions.)
- **Tertiary Color**: `#c55b00` (An accent color for highlights, badges, or decorative elements that need to stand out.)
- **Neutral Color**: `#74777f` (Provides a foundational base for backgrounds, surfaces, and non-chromatic UI components, ensuring visual balance.)

## Typography

A consistent typographic scale is used to establish hierarchy and readability. The `inter` font family is chosen for all textual elements.

- **Headline Font**: inter
- **Body Font**: inter
- **Label Font**: inter

## Shape and Form

The visual characteristics of UI elements are defined by their roundedness.

- **Roundedness**: 2 (Corners have a moderate curvature, providing a balanced and friendly aesthetic.)

## Spacing

The amount of whitespace is carefully controlled to ensure clarity and user comfort.

- **Spacing**: 2 (Normal spacing is applied, offering a comfortable visual density without feeling cramped or overly expansive.)
